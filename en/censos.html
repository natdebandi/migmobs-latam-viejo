Censuses: population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia

How many and who

Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia

People enumerated whose country of birth is Venezuela or Bolivia. Argentina 2022 is read from the REDATAM database, which has 273,498 fewer people than the total published by INDEC.

Reading. In Argentina, people born in Venezuela went from 6,379 in 2010 to 161,495 in 2022; in Chile in 2024 they were 668,560.

Sources. INDEC (Argentina). Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2010 and 2022. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata (2010) and REDATAM (2022). INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.

Age and sex

Share of the population born in each country by age group and sex, over the total of that population in each census. Men on the left, women on the right.

Reading. 47% of the people born in Venezuela enumerated in Argentina in 2022 were aged 25 to 39 (47% in 2010).

Reading. 32% of the people born in Bolivia enumerated in Argentina in 2022 were aged 25 to 39; in Chile in 2024, 40%.

Sources. INDEC (Argentina). Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2010 and 2022. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata (2010) and REDATAM (2022). INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.

Where and since when

Where they live

Population born in each country by province of residence (Argentina, 2010 and 2022 censuses) and by region (Chile, 2024 census). The ten jurisdictions with the largest population of that origin.

Reading. Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires has 84,834 people born in Venezuela in Argentina 2022 (3,002 in 2010). In Chile, the region with the most is Metropolitana de Santiago (441,568).

Reading. Buenos Aires has 161,935 people born in Bolivia in Argentina 2022 (147,781 in 2010). In Chile, the region with the most is Antofagasta (48,856).

Sources. INDEC (Argentina). Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2010 and 2022. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata (2010) and REDATAM (2022). INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.

When they arrived

Year of arrival reported by the population born in each origin still living in the country on census day. People who arrived and left are not counted. Argentina from 1980; Chile by periods.

Reading. 83% of the people born in Venezuela enumerated in Argentina in 2022 arrived in 2017 or later. Peak year: 2018 (46,881 people).

Reading. 8% of the people born in Bolivia enumerated in Argentina in 2022 arrived in 2017 or later. Peak year: 2007 (11,502 people).

Sources. INDEC (Argentina). Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2010 and 2022. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata (2010) and REDATAM (2022). INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.

Education and work

Highest level of education completed

Distribution by highest level of education completed, excluding missing data and special education. Levels harmonised across the three censuses; Chile only records completed levels. All ages.

Reading. 47% of the population born in Venezuela enumerated in Argentina in 2022 had completed tertiary education, and 36% in Chile in 2024.

Reading. 7% of the population born in Bolivia enumerated in Argentina in 2022 had completed tertiary education, and 9% in Chile in 2024.

Sources. INDEC (Argentina). Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2010 and 2022. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata (2010) and REDATAM (2022). INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.

Activity and unemployment

Activity rate: employed plus unemployed over the population aged 14+ (Argentina) or 15+ (Chile). Unemployment rate: unemployed over employed plus unemployed. Missing data excluded.

Reading. In Argentina in 2022, 79% of women born in Venezuela (aged 14 and over) were working or looking for work; among men, 89%.

Reading. In Argentina in 2022, 62% of women born in Bolivia (aged 14 and over) were working or looking for work; among men, 82%.

Sources. INDEC (Argentina). Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2010 and 2022. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata (2010) and REDATAM (2022). INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.

Birth and nationality

Chile 2024: country of birth and nationality

Cross-tabulation of country of birth and reported nationality in the 2024 Chile census. Argentina does not ask about nationality, so the whole analysis uses country of birth.

Reading. In Chile in 2024 there were 668,560 people born in Venezuela and 653,467 with Venezuelan nationality; 29,100 were born in Venezuela but reported another nationality.

Source. INE (Chile). Censo de Población y Vivienda 2024. Population born in Venezuela and in Bolivia; own processing of microdata.
